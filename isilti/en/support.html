FAQ

  • What is the Daily Course? Everyone flies the same course each day; you get 3 tries.
  • Who are the ghosts? Recordings of your own best flight, your friends and the day's top players.
  • How do I add a friend? Menu → Friends → enter their 6-character code.
  • Too hard or too easy? Settings → Wing feel: Gentle or Agile.
