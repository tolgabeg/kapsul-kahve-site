FAQ

How do I change the app language?
Profile → Settings → Language.

I forgot my password.
Profile → Continue with email → Forgot password. Open the link in the email on your iPhone.

How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete my account. Your profile and reviews are permanently deleted.

How do I report a review?
The "…" next to a review → Report review or Block this member.

My coffee isn't in the catalog.
Shelf → + → Take a photo of the box, or Add manually.
