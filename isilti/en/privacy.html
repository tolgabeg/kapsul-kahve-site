Privacy Policy

Işıltı · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Summary

Işıltı does not track you, does not sell your data and never asks for your email or real name. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one for extra tries.

Stored on your device

  • Your records, streak, spores, missions, badges and chosen looks
  • Your settings and friend list
  • Your own best daily flight recording

Sent to our server (Supabase)

  • Anonymous player ID: for online ghosts the app creates an anonymous account automatically. No email, phone or name needed.
  • Nickname and friend code: the default name looks like "Firefly 123"; if you change it, only friends who added your code see it.
  • Your Daily Course flight: the height recording of your best flight of the day, score and spore count. Shown to other players as an unnamed "ghost", and to your friends with your name.
  • Anonymous analytics (optional): at which gate and why a flight ended, the area, wing setting and app version. No player ID attached.
  • Crash reports (optional): technical reports generated on device by Apple MetricKit. No personal information.

Turn analytics and crash reports off in Settings → "Anonymous analytics and crash reports".

Ads (Google AdMob)

In the Daily Course you can optionally watch a rewarded ad for extra tries. Ads are served by Google AdMob; Google may process device information, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interactions to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app does not ask for tracking permission (ATT) and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EEA and UK you are asked for consent before ads, and you can change it in Settings → Ad privacy options. Details: Google's privacy policy.

Live Race

In a live room your nickname and flight position are relayed in real time only to players in that room; they are not stored.

Game Center

Leaderboards run on Apple Game Center and follow Apple's privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your data

Settings → "Delete my online data" permanently removes your anonymous account, name, friend code and flight recordings from the server. Anonymous analytics are not linked to you, so they cannot be deleted individually.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
