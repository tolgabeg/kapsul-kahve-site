Kapsül Kahve

A personal assistant for your coffee capsule shelf.

Tracks the capsules on your shelf, warns you before they run out and suggests a coffee for the time of day and your taste. Includes hot and cold recipes, brewing stats, badges and monthly spending. Optional membership lets you rate and review coffees.
