Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Summary

Kapsül Kahve does not track you across apps and does not sell your data. Your shelf, brew history, spending, ratings and notes are stored on your device; if you join a shared household shelf, the shelf and brews are shared with its members via Supabase. Optional rewarded ads are shown only for creating/joining a household and for viewing activity again after the daily free view.

Stored on your device

  • Your coffee shelf, stock, brew history, favorites, ratings and tasting notes
  • Box prices and your shopping list
  • App settings (language, theme, machine)
  • Box photos you take

None of this is sent to a server. Text on photos is read on the device.

If you become a member

Membership is optional and only needed for ratings/reviews in the catalog and a profile. If you join, the following is stored with our service provider Supabase:

  • Your email address (for sign-in; never shown to other members) or your Sign in with Apple identifier (Apple lets you hide your email)
  • Your display name and optional city, bio, favorite drink and profile photo (visible to other members)
  • Your ratings and reviews (public) and any reports you send

Barcode lookup

When you scan an unknown barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to find product details. No personal information is sent.

Rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

You may be asked to watch a short ad to create a household, join one, or open the activity a second time on the same day. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app does not request tracking permission (ATT) and does not use your advertising identifier (IDFA). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads. To verify a watched ad, only your account's technical ID and a one-time reward code are sent to Google. Details: How Google uses data.

Notifications

Stock, streak and descaling reminders are created on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete my account permanently deletes your account, profile, photo and all your reviews from the server. Your shelf stays on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
